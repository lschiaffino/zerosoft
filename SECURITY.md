# Security policy

Please **do not open public issues for security problems.**

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@zerosoft.ai** with a description, steps to reproduce and the impact. We aim to
acknowledge within 72 hours, keep you posted while we fix it, and credit you in the release notes
unless you prefer otherwise. We do not currently run a paid bug bounty.

To report phishing, malware or other abusive short links or pages (not a vulnerability in
Zerosoft itself), write to **abuse@zerosoft.ai** instead.

## Scope

Supported versions: the `main` branch and the hosted cloud at zerosoft.ai. Self-hosted instances
are operated by whoever runs them; report issues in the code to us, and issues in a specific
deployment to its operator.

## Rules of engagement

- Test only against your own accounts and data. Do not access, modify or delete other people's data.
- Do not degrade the service (no denial-of-service, load testing or automated scanning at abusive
  rates), and do not use spam or social engineering.
- Give us a reasonable window to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

If you follow these rules and act in good faith, we will not pursue legal action against you for
your research.

See also the security page at https://zerosoft.ai/en/security.
