# Data Processing Agreement (template)

Template for customers of zerosoft cloud who need a DPA under the GDPR, UK GDPR, LGPD or
Ley 25.326. To sign one, write to privacy@zerosoft.ai with the legal name and address of your
company; we send this text filled in for countersignature. Last reviewed: 2026-11-09.

> Owner action: have a lawyer review this template once before first use, and fill in the
> legal entity details of zerosoft in section 1.

## 1. Parties and roles

- **Controller:** the customer named in the signature block ("Customer").
- **Processor:** [zerosoft legal entity, registered address] ("zerosoft").

This DPA forms part of the agreement under which Customer uses zerosoft cloud (the Terms of
Service at https://zerosoft.ai/terms) and applies to personal data that zerosoft processes on
Customer's behalf ("Customer Personal Data").

## 2. Details of processing (Annex I)

- **Subject matter and duration:** providing zerosoft cloud for the term of the agreement plus
  the deletion period in section 9.
- **Nature and purpose:** hosting, storing, transmitting, displaying and processing data as
  instructed through the product (CRM, communications, documents, scheduling, analytics,
  automation and AI features the Customer chooses to use).
- **Data subjects:** Customer's users, employees, contractors, customers, leads, website
  visitors, candidates and any other individuals whose data Customer puts in the service.
- **Categories of data:** identification and contact data, communications content, files,
  usage and device data, and any data Customer chooses to upload. Customer should not upload
  special categories of data unless needed, and remains responsible for having a legal basis.

## 3. Instructions

zerosoft processes Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions (the
agreement, this DPA, and Customer's use and configuration of the product), unless required by
law, in which case it informs Customer first unless the law forbids it.

## 4. Confidentiality

Everyone at zerosoft authorized to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality.

## 5. Security (Annex II)

zerosoft implements the technical and organizational measures described at
https://zerosoft.ai/security and in its control matrix, including: encryption in transit
(TLS 1.2+) and at rest, field-level encryption of secrets with key rotation, role-based access
control, two-step verification, session management, audit logging, least-privilege internal
access with quarterly reviews, vulnerability scanning in CI, backups with restore tests, and an
incident response plan. zerosoft may update these measures if the level of protection does not
decrease.

## 6. Subprocessors

Customer gives general authorization for the subprocessors listed at
https://zerosoft.ai/subprocessors. zerosoft announces new subprocessors there at least 15 days
in advance; Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds, and if the parties
cannot resolve it, Customer may terminate the affected service. zerosoft imposes data
protection obligations on each subprocessor no less protective than this DPA and remains
liable for them.

## 7. Data subject requests and assistance

zerosoft provides self-service tools (export, deletion, editing) and, taking into account the
nature of processing, assists Customer with data subject requests, data protection impact
assessments and prior consultations. Requests received directly are forwarded to Customer.

## 8. Personal data breaches

zerosoft notifies Customer without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours after becoming
aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, with the information required
by GDPR art. 33(3) as it becomes available, and cooperates with Customer's investigation and
notifications.

## 9. Deletion and return

Customer can export its data at any time. On termination, Customer may export for 30 days;
then zerosoft deletes Customer Personal Data from production systems, and remaining copies in
backups expire within the backup retention period (at most 90 days in total), unless law
requires storage.

## 10. Audits

zerosoft makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance: this DPA, its
security documentation, and, once available, third-party audit reports (for example SOC 2) under
confidentiality. Where that is not sufficient, Customer may audit once a year on 30 days' notice,
during business hours, at its own cost, without access to other customers' data.

## 11. International transfers

Where Customer Personal Data is transferred outside the EEA, UK or Switzerland to a country
without an adequacy decision, the Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2 controller to
processor, or Module 3 processor to processor) adopted by Commission Decision (EU) 2021/914
are incorporated by reference, with the UK Addendum where applicable. Clause 7 (docking) applies;
clause 9 option 2 (general authorization); clause 11 optional language does not apply; clause 17
and 18: law and courts of Ireland. Annexes I and II are sections 2 and 5 of this DPA.

## 12. Liability and order of precedence

Liability is governed by the agreement. If this DPA conflicts with the agreement on data
protection, this DPA prevails; the Standard Contractual Clauses prevail over both.
