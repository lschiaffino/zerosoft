A 15-person company hires 10 people. The same month, with no new features, its SaaS bill goes up 67%. The product didn't change and the data barely grew. There are just more people logged in.

## What an extra user costs

For a typical SaaS, one more user is a row in the `users` table and a bit more bandwidth. The price goes up by a whole seat anyway: $30 per user per month for a project tool, $50 for a CRM.

A 50-person team pays $18,000 a year for a project tool whose infrastructure fits on a server that costs well under $100 a month. Almost all of that bill is margin, and it grows every time you hire.

## What it does to how teams work

- **Shared logins.** Five people use `sales@company.com`. The audit trail, permissions and per-person history go with it.
- **Teams left out.** The CRM stays sales-only because giving support access "costs too much", so support keeps a spreadsheet.
- **License chores.** Someone leaves and an admin rushes to remove their seat before renewal.

You pay for a collaboration tool and the price pushes you to collaborate less.

## What we charge for

Compute, storage and bandwidth cost money. A seat doesn't. So zerosoft doesn't charge for seats.

Self-hosting costs $0. On zerosoft Cloud you start on a free tier and then pay per workspace for usage: clicks, bookings, emails and storage. AI is billed by tokens at the provider's price ×1.2. Add the whole company; the bill changes when usage changes, not headcount.
