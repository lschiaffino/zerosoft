## What you can do without asking

- Say that your product is "based on zerosoft" or "a fork of zerosoft", or that
  it "works with zerosoft", as long as it's accurate and doesn't suggest we
  endorse it.
- Use the name to refer to the project in articles, talks, tutorials and
  reviews.
- Run an unmodified copy for your own organization and keep the name and logo
  in it.

## What needs a rebrand

If you distribute a modified version, or offer zerosoft or a fork of it as a
hosted service to others, you must:

- use a different name and logo that can't be confused with zerosoft;
- remove the zerosoft logo and zero mark from the interface, emails, the
  marketing pages and the app icons;
- not use "zerosoft" in your product name, domain name or social accounts.

You may keep the copyright and license notices, which the AGPLv3 requires.
